# Operator guide — SOM

Portal: no dedicated self-service e-procurement portal was
independently found by this iteration -- the Department of Public
Procurement (Ministry of Finance) is the national procurement
authority (never abbreviated "PPDA" anywhere this iteration read; see
`docs/business-model.md`). SBRLS (Small Business Registration &
Licensing System), MoCI's own one-stop business/company registration
system, is live at ebusiness.gov.so.
Entity: Certificate of Incorporation (Registrar of Companies, Ministry
of Commerce and Industry) + Tax Compliance Certificate (SHCN/TCC,
Ministry of Finance). Engagements with a natural-person owner holding
more than 10% of shares or de facto control additionally clear the
Beneficial Ownership Registration Regulation's own 30-working-day
Registrar-disclosure gate before a filing can proceed. Human-gated
filing only.
